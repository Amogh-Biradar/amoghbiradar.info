01 / THE MISSION

Start early. Teach by building. Leave a structure behind.

I founded STEM Champions after seeing how unevenly hands-on STEM opportunities were distributed. The goal was never a single event: it was a repeatable model for workshops, curricula, mentorship, and advocacy that partner organizations could carry forward.

My work has spanned program design, curriculum development, partnerships, volunteer leadership, fundraising, and the operational work of building a sustained nonprofit.

02 / EVOLUTION

From one initiative to durable infrastructure.
